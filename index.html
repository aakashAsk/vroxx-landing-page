Smart targets

Adaptive calorie targets that follow your real weight trend.

After five or more weigh-ins in 14 days and mostly complete meal logs, Vroxx compares your actual weight trend with your chosen pace. If they diverge, it recommends an updated daily calorie target.

You stay in control: every suggestion is yours to accept or dismiss, and Vroxx never changes your target without your approval.

Smart reminders

Smart fitness reminders that check your log first.

Vroxx reviews what you've already logged before it notifies you. If you've eaten lunch, there's no meal alert. If you're well behind on water, you get a single reminder. Every reminder can be switched off individually.
